Our process

Five steps, same order, every batch

  1. 1. Source

    We buy direct from four growing regions we've visited in person, at prices set above commodity rate.

  2. 2. Cup

    Every incoming lot is cupped blind against the last three harvests before it's approved for purchase.

  3. 3. Roast

    Small 12kg batches on our drum roaster, profiled per lot rather than run on a single house curve.

  4. 4. Rest

    Beans rest 24–72 hours to let CO2 escape before bagging, so the coffee doesn't taste flat or sharp.

  5. 5. Ship

    Bags go out within 48 hours of roasting — stamped with the roast date and batch number.
